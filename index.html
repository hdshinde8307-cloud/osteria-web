"The best moments in life are shared on a table over drinks and honest, delicious food."

Òsteria was born from a simple idea — that a cold beer, a warm pizza and good company are really all you need. Inspired by the easy charm of Mediterranean living and the bold flavours of Asian street kitchens, it's where crisp beers and cocktails, handmade pizzas and freshly made food come together in a laid-back, welcoming setting.

Every item on the menu is made from carefully sourced, fresh ingredients — simple recipes prepared with care and consistency, every time. Whether you're catching up with old friends or finding comfort in a quiet evening in, Òsteria feels like home from the very first visit.
